# ShopX Shipping Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-15 · Example document — replace or extend during Sprint 1._

## Delivery areas

ShopX delivers to all 34 provinces and cities of Vietnam. International shipping is not available.

## Delivery time

| Destination | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| Inside Ho Chi Minh City / Hanoi | 1–2 business days | Same day (orders before 11:00) |
| Other cities | 2–4 business days | 1–2 business days |
| Remote areas | 4–7 business days | Not available |

## Shipping fees

- Orders from 300,000 VND: free standard shipping.
- Orders under 300,000 VND: 25,000 VND standard shipping fee.
- Express shipping: 45,000 VND, regardless of order value.

## Carriers

Orders are delivered by GHN, GHTK, Viettel Post or J&T Express. The carrier is chosen automatically
and shown in the order details once the order is shipped.
