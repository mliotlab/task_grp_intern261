# Accepted Payment Methods

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## Supported Methods
ShopX accepts the following payment methods at checkout:
- Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible orders.
- Domestic ATM cards (Napas) and international credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB).
- E-wallets: MoMo, ZaloPay, and VNPay QR.
- Direct bank transfer via VietQR (confirmed automatically within 5 minutes).

## Failed Payments
- If an online payment fails or times out after 15 minutes, the order remains `pending` for 1 hour before automatic cancellation.
- Duplicate charges are refunded automatically within 3–7 business days depending on the issuing bank.
