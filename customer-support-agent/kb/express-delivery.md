# Express Delivery Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## Availability
Express delivery is available in Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, and major cities across Vietnam. It is not available in remote areas or for international addresses.

## Delivery Time
- Inside Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi: same-day delivery for orders placed before 11:00 (Monday to Saturday). Orders placed after 11:00 are delivered on the next business day.
- Other cities: 1–2 business days.

## Fees and Conditions
- Flat fee: 45,000 VND per order, regardless of order value (free shipping thresholds do not apply to express delivery).
- Not applicable to pre-order items or bulky appliances over 10 kg.
