# Account Registration and Verification

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## Creating an Account
- Customers can register on ShopX using a valid email address or Vietnamese mobile phone number.
- Each email address and phone number can only be linked to one active ShopX account.

## Verification Requirements
- A 6-digit OTP is sent via email or SMS and expires after 5 minutes.
- Verified email addresses are required for order status lookups and receiving e-invoices.

## Updating Account Information
- Shipping addresses can be edited anytime in Account Settings; changing the registered email requires OTP confirmation on both the old and new email.
