# Shipping Fees and Free Shipping

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## Standard Shipping Rates
- Orders under 300,000 VND: flat 25,000 VND standard shipping fee nationwide across all 34 provinces and cities of Vietnam.
- Express shipping: flat 45,000 VND, regardless of order value.

## Free Shipping Threshold
- Orders from 300,000 VND qualify for free standard shipping nationwide.
- Free standard shipping applies automatically at checkout and can be combined with one ShopX voucher.

## Surcharges
- Bulky items exceeding 15 kg or 60 cm on any side incur a 25,000 VND handling surcharge.
