# Cash on Delivery (COD) Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## Eligibility and Limits
- COD is available nationwide for orders with a total value under 5,000,000 VND.
- Orders from 5,000,000 VND and above require online prepayment (card, e-wallet, or bank transfer).

## Inspection Before Payment
- Customers may inspect the outer packaging and verify item quantity before paying the courier.
- Opening manufacturer-sealed electronic boxes or powering on devices before payment is not permitted.

## Refusal Policy
- Accounts with 3 consecutive unexcused COD refusals will have COD disabled for 30 days.
