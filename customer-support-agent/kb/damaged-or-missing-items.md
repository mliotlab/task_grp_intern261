# Damaged or Missing Items on Delivery

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## Inspection and Reporting Window
- Customers must report damaged parcels, broken seals, or missing items within 48 hours of the `delivered` timestamp.
- An unedited unboxing video clearly showing the shipping label and package contents is required for verification.

## Resolution
- Verified missing items are reshipped free of charge within 2 business days, or refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- Damaged electronics are eligible for immediate 1-to-1 replacement at no extra shipping cost.
