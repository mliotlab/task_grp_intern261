# Account Deletion and Data Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## How to Request Account Deletion
- Customers can submit a deletion request in Account Settings > Privacy or by creating a support ticket (`category: account`).
- Deletion cannot be processed while there are active orders (`pending`, `confirmed`, `shipped`) or open refund/warranty claims.

## Processing Time and Data Retention
- Verified deletion requests are completed permanently within 7 business days and cannot be undone.
- Transaction and VAT invoice records are retained for 5 years as required by Vietnamese accounting law, while personal profile data is anonymized.
