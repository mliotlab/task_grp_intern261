# E-Invoice and VAT Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## VAT Inclusion
All product prices displayed on ShopX already include 8% or 10% VAT according to current Vietnamese tax regulations.

## Requesting a Company E-Invoice
- Customers must select "Request VAT Invoice" at checkout and provide company name, tax ID (MST), registered address, and recipient email.
- Requests submitted after checkout are only accepted within 24 hours of order placement.

## Delivery of E-Invoice
- E-invoices (PDF and XML) are sent via email within 3 business days after the order status becomes `delivered`.
