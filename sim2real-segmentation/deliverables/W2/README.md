# Week 2 – Understand the method

**Goal:** explain the pipeline and main components of the chosen paper (DAFormer recommended).

| Member | Part |
|---|---|
| Member 1 | Inputs/outputs at training and inference time; redraw the overall pipeline; show source vs target data flow |
| Member 2 | List the main components/strategies; explain each one's role in 1–2 sentences (no proofs needed) |
| Member 3 | Datasets, metric, main results table, ablation table – which component contributes most? |

## Deliverables (Drive `/W2`)

1. **Methodology diagram** (`methodology.png` / `.pdf`) – redrawn by the team (draw.io, PowerPoint, …), every block annotated. **Do not** screenshot the paper's figure.
2. **Explanation (1–2 pages, PDF)** answering: What are the inputs/outputs? How does the method work? What is the role of each component? How do the authors evaluate it?

## Passes when

The diagram shows the correct source/target data flow and main blocks; every component's role is explained in our own words; the most important component according to the ablation is identified; **any member** can explain the whole diagram at check-in.
