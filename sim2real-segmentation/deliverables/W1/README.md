# Week 1 – Explore the topic

**Goal:** understand the problem and the main families of solutions. **Effort:** ≈ 5–6 h/person.

## Deliverables (due before Friday check-in, Drive folder `/W1`)

1. **3-paper summary table** (one shared file) – fill in [`summary_table.md`](summary_table.md).
2. **4 intro slides** – 1 common slide (problem, motivation, 2 solution directions) + 1 slide per paper.
3. **Weekly progress report** – include which paper the team picks for Week 2 and why.

## Passes when

- The problem is written in the team's own words (no copy from the abstract).
- Each paper lists at least one limitation.
- All 3 papers are compared on the same criteria; numbers are those reported by each paper.
- Citations are complete (authors, venue, year); every section names its owner.

## Fails if

- Text copied from the paper or abstract · a paper or the comparison is missing · owners not named.
